OUR HISTORY

A DECADE OF
BUILDING.

What started as a two-man field marketing operation out of West Jordan has become the Salt Lake Valley's most diversified residential services group. Every company in the portfolio was built from the ground up — no acquisitions, no outside capital.

We identify gaps in the residential market, build the operation, staff it with vetted personnel, and scale it with the same elite standards across every brand.

2016
Topaz Holdings Founded
Kenneth Tupea launches Topaz Field Marketing in West Jordan with a team of two. First contracts secured in home security D2D sales.
2018
Holding Structure Formalized
Topaz Holdings Group established as the parent entity. Team grows to 12 field reps across Salt Lake and Utah counties.
2019
CurbID Logistics Launched
First portfolio expansion. CurbID launches residential stenciling services across West Jordan and South Jordan neighborhoods.
2021
Luminary Lights & Wasatch Property Care
Portfolio doubles. Two new seasonal and exterior service brands launch simultaneously, filling year-round demand gaps.
2023
Alder Residential & Apex Mobile Detail
Most ambitious year yet. Two new brands launch within 90 days of each other. Alder Residential rapidly becomes the flagship consumer-facing company.
2026
Six Companies. Scaling Valley-Wide.
Topaz Holdings operates six active portfolio companies across the Salt Lake Valley with full coverage from Park City to Herriman.
